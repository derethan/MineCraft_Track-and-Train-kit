## 2. Critical Developer Guidelines & Gotchas
1. **Always Check and Correct User Spelling**: Maintain strict spelling adherence in responses and documentation (e.g. "Surveyor's Charge", "Remote Detonator", "borehole", "signal", etc.).
2. **UTF-8 WITHOUT BOM**: javac fails with illegal character: '\ufeff' if files contain a UTF-8 BOM. When generating or editing Java files in PowerShell, always use New-Object System.Text.UTF8Encoding False.
3. **PowerShell Variable Expansion**: Do not pass complex inline scripts containing $ or double quotes via cmd /c powershell -Command "..." because Windows CLI strips $. Always save scripts into scratch/*.ps1 and run via powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File "path".
4. **Recipe Categories**: Vanilla 1.21.4 CraftingBookCategory enum only accepts "building", "equipment", "misc", or "redstone". Setting category to "transportation" crashes world loading.
5. **Documentation Sync**: When making custom content changes, always keep WIKI.md, README.md, CURSEFORGE.md, and conversation walkthroughs updated.

## 4. Train Chaining & Physics Engine (TrainChainingUtil.java)
- **Railroad Crowbar**: Right-click cart A then cart B to couple with heavy steel chain. Shift+Right-Click to uncouple.
- **Coupler Radiuses**: Heavy Diesel (1.18m), Tanker DOT-111 (1.12m), Caboose (0.95m), Livestock/Turret/Cargo/Electric (0.88m), Steam (0.78m), Small Tanker (0.75m), Vanilla (0.50m).
- **Locomotive Connection-Point Pushing & Shunting**:
  - Locomotives push carts from their exact connection points (coupler bumpers) forward or reverse:
    Target contact distance = locoCouplerRadius + cartCouplerRadius (e.g. 2.06m for Diesel + Cargo).
  - When shunting, locomotives push unchained carts along the rails ahead of the bumper, preventing carts from clipping or wedging inside the locomotive hood/cab.
  - Cowcatcher mob deflection pushes wandering mobs and players forward and to the side with an upward hop without stalling.
  - Solid voxel collision between locomotives and carts is decoupled while bumper physics maintains separation.
- **Rolling Stock Push & Bumping Physics**:
  - Non-locomotive rolling stock carts use pushable momentum physics so players can push them by hand to position them for coupling.
  - Carts exchange momentum naturally when bumped by locomotives or rolling into other carts.
  - Chained carts ignore vanilla push forces against each other to allow smooth consist motion without jitter.
- **Multi-Block Grade & Slope Physics**:
  - Deep 5-block vertical rail scanning: getCurrentBlockPosOrRailBelow(Entity) checks Y, Y-1, Y-2, Y-3, Y+1 to prevent freezing in mid-air over 45Â° descending slope staircases.
  - Safe Sweep Pre-Lifting: y = railPos.getY() + 1.0 during horizontal move() sweep ensures train collision boxes clear the stone step blocks supporting the track bed at the bottom of inclines.
  - Guarded Slope Exit Stepping: if (shape.isSlope()) steps down Y - 1.0 onto descending continuation rails below while never executing phantom hops on flat track.

## 6. Geological Prospecting & Survey System
- **Surveyor's Charge (SurveyorsChargeBlock)**:
  - **Crafting Recipe**: 1 Redstone Dust + 1 TNT + 1 Arrow (shapeless, "redstone" category).
  - **Detonation Trigger**: Triggered by redstone signal or wirelessly via Remote Detonator.
  - **Detonation Effects**: Self-destructs instantly with loud explosion sound (GENERIC_EXPLODE) and smoke particles (EXPLOSION_EMITTER, CAMPFIRE_COSY_SMOKE).
  - **Borehole Excavation**: Blasts a clean 1x1 vertical borehole straight down from its position until hitting bedrock or indestructible blocks (destroySpeed < 0.0F). Configurable block dropping.
  - **Geological Survey Scan**: Scans a 10-block radius in all directions (configurable) around the charge. Tallies mineral/block quantities sorted descending.
  - **Oil Deposit Detection**: Automatically detects and tallies crude oil / fuel fluids (c:oil, c:crude_oil, registry path match) and oil blocks, highlighting total deposits and source reservoirs in gold.
  - **Config Options (Config.java)**:
